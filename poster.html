Core Research Focus

Key Areas
T1

Digital Twin and Vision Perception-based Autonomous Swarm of Flying robots

Researching distributed SLAM, vision-based perception, and cooperative control for autonomous flying robot swarms with real-time digital twins.

T2

MR/XR-based Virtual Cockpit for IoT-connected Unmanned Vehicles

Developing immersive mixed and extended reality (MR/XR) virtual cockpits for ultra-low latency remote teleoperation over IoT wireless networks.

T3

Adaptive Shared Autonomy for Precise Control of Aerial Manipulators in Contact-Rich Missions

Pioneering adaptive shared autonomy frameworks uniting human teleoperation and onboard autonomous stabilization for aerial manipulators during physical contact tasks.

T4

Dynamic Coupling Compensation for Precise Positioning of Aerial Manipulators in Industrial Contact Tasks

Formulating multi-body dynamic coupling compensation models and dynamic center-of-mass balancing to reject disturbance and ensure high-precision industrial contact operations.
