Core Research Focus

Autonomous UAV Swarms

Researching advanced perception, mapping, and coordination for swarms of UAVs utilizing digital twin technology for real-time monitoring.

Constrained Communications

Developing robust networking protocols (e.g., Lora, NB-IoT) tailored for low-power edge nodes in demanding environments.

Digital Cockpit & VR

Designing immersive virtual cockpits for remote operation of ground and aerial vehicles using AR/VR over 5G.

Hardware-in-the-Loop

Validating complex cyber-physical systems and real-time control algorithms using state-of-the-art HIL platforms.
