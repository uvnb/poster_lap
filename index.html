Introduction

The Embedded Networking Research Group investigates how constrained devices — sensors, microcontrollers, and edge nodes — communicate reliably under tight limits on power, bandwidth, and compute. We work across the stack, from radio-layer protocol design to distributed coordination across thousands of nodes.

Our approach combines systems building, formal analysis, and real-world deployment: every protocol we design is implemented on real hardware and tested in the field, not just simulated.

Core Research Focus

SWARM ROBOTICS

Digital Twin/Vision Perception-based Autonomous Swarm of UAV-embedded Robots

Researching advanced perception, mapping, and coordination for swarms of UAVs utilizing digital twin technology for real-time monitoring and control.

IOT & VEHICLES

Virtual Cockpit Construction in IoT-connected Unmanned Vehicles

Developing immersive, responsive, and robust virtual cockpits for remote operation and monitoring of unmanned vehicles over latency-constrained IoT networks.

AI & AUTONOMY

AI-Driven Autonomous Decision and Learning in Flying Robots

Applying machine learning and AI algorithms for robust, real-time autonomous decision making in resource-constrained flying robots in dynamic environments.
