Vision & Mission

We investigate how constrained devices — sensors, microcontrollers, and edge nodes — communicate reliably under tight limits on power, bandwidth, and compute.

By bridging the gap between theoretical communications and practical embedded systems, we enable scalable, intelligent, and real-time operations for unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) and IoT networks.

Core Research Focus

Developing robust algorithms and hardware-software co-designs for next-generation networked systems.

Autonomous UAV Swarms

Researching advanced perception, mapping, and coordination for swarms of UAVs utilizing digital twin technology for real-time monitoring.

Virtual Cockpit for IoT

Developing immersive, responsive virtual cockpits for remote operation of unmanned vehicles over latency-constrained wireless networks.

AI-Driven Autonomy

Applying edge machine learning and reinforcement learning algorithms for robust, real-time decision making in flying robots.

Prospective Members

What you will gain and what we expect when you join our Lab.

Benefits

  • 🎉 Experience collaborative projects with enterprises and international partners.
  • 🎉 Foster creative capacity, scientific thinking methodologies, and essential professional skills.
  • 🎉 Access to modern equipment, machinery, components, and technical facilities.
  • 🎉 Connect with an extensive alumni network comprising business leaders, entrepreneurs, R&D experts, and prestigious researchers. Many successful alumni are currently residing in the US, Canada, Japan, South Korea, and Western Europe.
  • 🎉 Receive guidance and support in seeking postgraduate scholarships locally and internationally, as well as employment opportunities at major corporations like Toshiba, LG, Viettel, Samsung, VNPT TECH, Vin, etc.
  • 🎉 Engage in the Lab's extracurricular activities to develop social skills.

Requirements

  • 📌 Be a student in Electrical, Mechanical, Electronics, Telecommunications, or Information Technology engineering.
  • 📌 Proficiency in coding with at least one programming language (C/C++, Java, Python, etc.).
  • 📌 Ability to read and comprehend English technical documentation at a basic level or higher.
  • 📌 Possess a strong eagerness to learn, high self-study capability, and a robust motivation for research.
